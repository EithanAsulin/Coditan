# Coditan Skills
Think of Coditan skills like magic, they allow Coditan to do more than it was designed for.

## What can Coditan skills do?
Coditan skills are `Python-based scripts` that can use external libraries to expand the feature-set of Coditan with libraries like translators, popups or system-level actions.

Coditan skills may also just be instructions, they may be instructions for how to design better or specific info Coditan should know!

## Why use Coditan skills?
Coditan skills have a simple job, **Reduce the time it takes to do something** it's like using pre-built shortcuts on the way to reach the final result faster
